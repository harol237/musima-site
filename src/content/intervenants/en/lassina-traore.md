Lassina Traore is an entrepreneur with experience in building business projects, teaching, and starting initiatives aimed at young people and entrepreneurs. He is CEO of Zentra, a business first specialising in mobility and vehicle services, and CEO of Nexuspreneur, an ecosystem set up to connect, back and support businesses and personal projects.

He is also the founder of LT Consulting, an initiative focused on financial education, personal finance and investment. Since 2019 he has been teaching and writing about finance, financial markets and blockchain.

His path combines a solid technical and industrial grounding with specialised training in private banking, financial advice, financial markets, fintech and blockchain. His approach comes down to one thing: turning knowledge and experience into practical projects, where entrepreneurship, education and the creation of opportunities meet.
